# Name research: The Great Battle Gaiden 2 (Matsuri da Wasshoi)

Ruling: official English where it exists, else Hepburn without macrons. "AGTP" = Aeon Genesis patches
for SD The Great Battle / Great Battle II-V (2018). AGTP spellings were read from their patch zips
(aeongenesis.net/download/tgb1..tgb5): the readmes plus the English strings inside the IPS files.

Sources:
- [JA-WP] https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ザ・グレイトバトル外伝2_祭りだワッショイ
- [GC] Game Catalog @Wiki https://w.atwiki.jp/gcmatome/pages/6033.html (character, boss and enemy list)
- [SIMPLE] review https://sinplelove.jp/blog-entry-3115.html
- [EN-WP] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compati_Hero
- [AGTP] https://aeongenesis.net/projects/tgb4 (and tgb1/2/3/5)
- [PIXIV] https://dic.pixiv.net/a/エミィ(コンパチヒーロー)

Official retail title kana: 祭**り**だワッショイ (some listings drop the り). Story: 「フェスティバル大帝」 invades the
「コンパチワールド祭り」 [JA-WP][GC].
